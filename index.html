🎯 Learning Objectives (Tujuan Pembelajaran)

By the end of this module, I can:

  1. use regular and irregular verbs, adjectives, nouns, and time expressions to talk about my activities. (menggunakan kosakata untuk menceritakan kegiatan)
  2. explain the patterns of present and past tense, verbal and nonverbal. (menjelaskan pola kalimat)
  3. build positive, negative, and question sentences in Simple Past Tense with a model. (menyusun kalimat dengan model)
  4. write 2–3 sentences about my last Sunday or holiday. (menulis pengalaman sendiri)
  5. tell my story to a friend with confidence. (menceritakan dengan percaya diri)

🌈 About This Module

Flow: VOCABULARY → GRAMMAR → MODEL → SENTENCE → WRITING → SPEAKING.

DUP / UDL

Engagement: individual practice, pair work, team game, challenge, reflection.
Representation: vocabulary tables, pictures, sentence patterns, examples, audio, interactive activities.
Action & Expression: matching, arranging, correcting, writing, speaking, game.

TaRL

Check My Level gives a starting point (Word Explorer, Sentence Builder, Sentence Creator), not a permanent label. Everyone can move to another level at any time.

💡 Why This Module? (TPACK)

Content: vocabulary, present/past tense, verbal/nonverbal sentences, writing, speaking.
Pedagogy: scaffolding, modeling, guided practice, differentiation, gamification, peer learning.
Technology: interactive web module, audio, digital quiz, game, progress tracking.

Technology is used because it has a teaching function (instant feedback, audio, replay), not as decoration.

🌱 Deep Learning

Mindful (Berkesadaran): Check My Level, self-assessment, reflection.
Meaningful (Bermakna): writing about my own weekend, contextual vocabulary.
Joyful (Menggembirakan): Snake & Ladder, quizzes, challenges, badges.

Mistakes are part of learning.

A Short Profile

Salsa Nur Fadilla, S.Pd., is a pre-service teacher in the Pre-Service Teacher Professional Education Program (PPG Prajabatan) at Universitas Nahdlatul Ulama Surabaya. She holds a Bachelor of Education (S.Pd.) and focuses on English language teaching at the elementary school level.

During her teaching practice at SDN Siwalankerto 1 Surabaya, she worked with Grade VI learners and observed a consistent learning need: students could follow grammar rules after an explanation, yet they struggled to build their own sentences because their English vocabulary was limited and they lacked models to imitate. This classroom evidence became the basis of the English Pocket Module, whose flow moves from vocabulary, to grammar, to a model, to a sentence, and finally to writing and speaking.

Her academic interests include inclusive and differentiated instruction, Universal Design for Learning (UDL), Teaching at the Right Level (TaRL), deep learning, TPACK-based instructional media, and game-based learning. Alongside this module, she has developed vocabulary pocket books, learning games, and lesson plans aligned with the Kurikulum Merdeka.

She believes that every child can learn English when given meaningful models, supportive scaffolding, and a safe space to make mistakes.
